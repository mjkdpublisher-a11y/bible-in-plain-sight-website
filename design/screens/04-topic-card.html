Money worries

When Money Is Tight

7 min read · Scripture from the Berean Standard Bible (BSB)
Opening

You are not alone in this

If you are reading this with a knot in your stomach, take a slow breath. Worrying about money does not mean you have failed God, and it does not mean he has stopped caring for you.

Bills arrive whether we are ready or not. The numbers don't add up, and the mind runs ahead to next month and the month after that. Scripture never pretends this weight is small. It was written for people who knew debt, hunger and uncertain harvests.

Lament

It's okay to say it

Before any answer, the Bible gives us permission to be honest. The psalms are full of people telling God exactly how hard things are (Psalm 13 is one). You can do the same, in your own words:

Lord, I'm tired of counting. I'm afraid of what the next letter will say, and I can't see how this works out.

Honest words like these are not a lack of faith. They are a form of it: you are bringing the problem to Someone who listens.

Scripture

What God says

A story from Scripture

Bread for one day

When Israel crossed the wilderness, there were no fields and no storehouses. Each morning God gave manna, enough for that day. Those who tried to keep extra overnight found it spoiled by morning (Exodus 16:20).

It must have felt fragile, living one morning at a time. Yet every morning, it came. Centuries later, Jesus taught his followers to pray, “Give us this day our daily bread” (Matthew 6:11).

Practical step

One step for today

Pick one small, concrete thing and do it today.

  • Open the envelope you've been avoiding.
  • Write every bill on a single page.
  • Call someone who can help: a church deacon, a free debt advice service, or a trusted friend.

You don't need to solve the whole month. Only today's portion.

Prayer

Pray with me

Father, you know what I need before I ask. I bring you the numbers that frighten me and the future I can't see. Give me this day my daily bread, and the wisdom to take the next right step. Guard my heart with your peace, and help me not to carry tomorrow before it comes. In Jesus' name, amen.

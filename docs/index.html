Scripture for real life

What the Bible says about what you are actually facing.

Money worries, grief, anxiety, conflict, doubt and more. Each topic offers relevant Bible passages with context, a short reflection, a prayer, and a place to write down how things change over time.

Scripture quotations are from the Berean Standard Bible (BSB), public domain.

How it works

From a hard moment to a word you can hold on to.

  1. Step 1

    Say what you are facing

    90 topics in 10 categories, from money worries and stress at work to grief, doubt and joy.

  2. Step 2

    Read Scripture in context

    Passages with a note on their context. Every passage opens the whole chapter, so no verse is read out of place.

  3. Step 3

    Pray, write, take a step

    A prayer, one practical step, a verse to keep, and a journal with a simple “How heavy does it feel?” scale.

  4. Step 4

    See how it changed

    A few days later the app asks, “How is it now?” Your Timeline shows the way you have come.

Inside the app

Calm to read. Easy to come back to.

Warm paper, large clear type, and help that is always one tap away.

For someone I love

Turn any topic toward a person you care about. The prayer becomes a prayer for them, by name, with tips on how to be there for them. You can send them a verse as an image.

Stones of Remembrance

Short notes, in your own words, of a time God helped you, so you can find them again on a harder day.

Need help now?

If you are in danger, call your local emergency number.

In the app, “I need help now” is always one tap away. It shows helplines for your country, or a way to find one. You can also find a helpline at findahelpline.com.

Bible in Plain Sight offers Scripture, prayer and a place to write. It does not replace a doctor, a therapist or emergency services.

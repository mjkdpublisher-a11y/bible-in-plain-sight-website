Before you begin

A few things
you might wonder.

Have another question?
We're here to help.

Can I download the app now?

Bible in Plain Sight is available for Android on Google Play. Use any download button on this page to open the app listing.

What can I use for free?

18 topics are free forever, including all five crisis topics. Every app feature is free. One optional purchase unlocks the remaining 72 topics. There is no subscription.

Who can read my journal?

Your journal is encrypted and stored on your phone. We never see it. You can export or delete your entries at any time. No one monitors your journal or check-in answers.

Can I pray for someone else?

Yes. “For someone I love” turns a topic into a prayer for them, by name, with suggestions for how to support them. You can also share a verse as an image.

What happens if I change my phone?

Your journal is stored locally, with no cloud backup. Before changing or resetting your phone, export your data to keep a copy for your records. Exported files cannot be loaded back into the app. Find more information on the support page.

Which Bible translation does the app use?

Scripture quotations use the Berean Standard Bible (BSB), which is in the public domain. Passages come with context, and you can open the whole chapter.

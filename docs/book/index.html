Before you choose.

A few answers about reading and editions.

Do I need to read from beginning to end?

No. Start with a topic that matters to you, follow the “Find what you need” guide, or read through at your own pace.

Do I need the Bible in Plain Sight app?

No. The book can be read on its own. The app is optional and offers a separate place for Scripture, prayer, and private reflection.

What is the difference between Kindle and the Ko-fi eBook?

The Kindle edition is purchased through Amazon for reading with Kindle. The Ko-fi offer provides an eBook file in EPUB format for compatible readers, plus the additional interactive PDF. Choose Kindle if you prefer to manage your reading through Amazon.

Is the PDF only for printing?

No. It is also made for reading on screen, with clickable contents and links to help you move through the book. Its US Letter pages can be printed if you prefer.

Who is the book for?

Adults who have followed Christ for years and those beginning to explore Christian faith. It brings everyday concerns into conversation with Scripture, prayer, and reflection.

Pray for Anna

Father, I bring Anna to You. You know the pressure Anna is carrying about money, and You see every bill and every sleepless night. Give Anna peace for today, wisdom for the decisions ahead, and people who will help in practical ways. Show me how I can help too. In Jesus' name, amen.

For someone I love

How to be there for them

Money worries often come with shame. Listen more than you advise, and don't compare their situation to anyone else's. Ask if there's something practical you can do, like helping them write down the numbers or look for free debt advice.

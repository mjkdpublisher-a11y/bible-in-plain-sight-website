Scripture

What God says

Matthew 6:25–26
“

Therefore I tell you, do not worry about your life, what you will eat or drink; or about your body, what you will wear. Is not life more than food, and the body more than clothes? Look at the birds of the air: They do not sow or reap or gather into barns, and yet your heavenly Father feeds them. Are you not much more valuable than they?”

Jesus says this in the Sermon on the Mount, right after warning that no one can serve both God and money. He isn't asking people to stop working. He's asking them to stop carrying a weight that belongs to their Father.

Matthew 6:34
“

Therefore do not worry about tomorrow, for tomorrow will worry about itself. Today has enough trouble of its own.”

Much of our anxiety lives in next month, next year, "what if." Jesus brings us back to today, the only day we're actually asked to live.

Philippians 4:6–7
“

Be anxious for nothing, but in everything, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God. And the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus.”

Paul wrote this letter while held as a prisoner, dependent on the gifts of friends. The promise is peace, not an instant solution: God guards your heart even before the situation changes.

Philippians 4:19
“

And my God will supply all your needs according to His glorious riches in Christ Jesus.”

Paul says this to a church that had sent him money even though they were poor themselves. The promise is about needs, not everything we might want, and it rests on God's faithfulness, not on our bank balance.

Hebrews 13:5
“

Keep your lives free from the love of money and be content with what you have, for God has said: “Never will I leave you, never will I forsake you.””
